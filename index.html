General Dentistry

Complete diagnosis of dental problems after thorough clinical examination and investigations. Dental Heaven will make your smile better. Treatment of presenting complaint on priority basis.

Cosmetic Dentistry

According to your needs we will provide you with Dental Veeners, Composite restorations, Tooth whitening, Crowns and other procedures that may alter the size of your teeth to best match your smile.

Teeth Whitening

Get your teeth a model look after getting our Tooth Whitening session. Tooth whitening is done in clinic through bleaching. We also provide home applied bleaching equipment on demand.

Orthodontics

Dental treatment for malaligned teeth to give you the best smile. Dental braces and other related appliances are given as a part of treatment to bring your teeth to ideal position and give you an ideal smile

Dental Implants

Replacement of missing teeth with dental implants without causing any damage to other teeth. Dental implant is surgically placed in bone and gives high strength and durability to the artificial tooth.

Pedodontics

Special care of child patient with dental problems. 1st visit of a child to the dental clinic should be at the age of 6 months. Regular dental visits prevent the child from any dental diseases.

Endodontics

Treatment of teeth with dental pain due to deep caries or injury through root canal treatment. Treatment of teeth with previously failed root canal treatment.

Dental Surgery

Removal of hopeless teeth by extraction. Removal of impacted teeth. Removal of teeth is done by: Simple extraction Surgical extraction

Prosthodontics

Artificial teeth to replace missing natural teeth. It includes: Removable cast partial dentures Removable full dentures Fixed teeth by crown and bridge work Dental Implants
