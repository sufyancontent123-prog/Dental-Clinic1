Patient safety is paramount. We follow rigorous 4-stage Class-B hospital autoclave sterilization. Every single surgical instrument is vacuum-sealed and opened fresh in front of the patient. Disposable barrier films and examination kits are used without compromise.
Clear aligners are virtually invisible, custom-molded transparent trays that gently straighten crooked teeth without wires or brackets. They can be easily removed while eating and brushing. With proper oral hygiene, routine dental checkups, and experienced surgical placement (such as performed by our Oral Surgeon Doctor 1), titanium dental implants have a clinical success rate of over 98% and can easily last a lifetime.
Thanks to our modern rotary endodontic equipment and apex locators, most routine root canal treatments at Innova Dental are completed in a single comfortable visit of 45–60 minutes. Complicated infections may require a second visit to ensure complete biological healing.
